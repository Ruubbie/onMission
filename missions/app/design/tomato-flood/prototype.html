Tomato flood · clickable prototype

One idea
per screen

Every tab and every page leads with one big thing on tomato: days to go, money raised, one prayer, how packed your bag is. Everything else sits on a calm cream sheet with no more than three or four items. Motion does the talking: when you do something, the screen answers.

  • The new tab bar

    The active tab grows into a tomato pill with its name, and the others shrink to icons. It springs across when you switch, and the icon plays its little move. The bar takes the colour of the page behind it, with no line between them. A dot marks a tab that needs you.

  • Pages grow out of what you tap

    Tap a tile, a person or a task and its page floods out of that exact spot. Back folds it into where it came from.

  • After a call

    Slide how it went. The whole screen shifts from night through sunrise to full sun, and the word changes with it. Saving moves Anna along her path.

  • Pray, and a candle lights

    Tap I prayed: the candle lights, the count ticks up and the next prayer slides in. Swipe to skip one. Answered floods the screen gold.

  • The + grows into the page

    The + spins into a close button and the page floods out of it. It guesses what you're adding while you type, then folds back into the +.

  • Ticking things off

    The check draws itself and the row slides away. When you clear the day, the cream sheet drops and the tomato takes the whole screen.

Every page

Each one has its own moment: the suitcase fills, a SOLD stamp lands, the scale tips, the letter flies off.

In the app these use spring animations and haptics, and everything falls back to simple fades with Reduce Motion.
