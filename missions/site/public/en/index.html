Read my story

Why I’m going to the other side of the world.

I’m Ruben. In January 2027 I fly to Queenstown, New Zealand, to serve full time with Youth With A Mission (YWAM). My faith grew step by step, and my DTS there changed the most: I learned so much about God, the Bible and who I am. Here’s the short version of what comes next.

  1. The place

    I know YWAM Queenstown from my DTS, and it’s a beautiful place. This isn’t a choice I pulled out of my pocket: it’s a calling I received after weeks of prayer and seeking, and God confirmed it through the people who prayed with me.

  2. The people

    It hurts me how many people my age believe lies about who they are and about God’s love. Many of the young people live on the base, so discipleship doesn’t stop at five o’clock: it happens over games in the evening, at dinner and on the ordinary days.

  3. The plan

    I’ll look after the base’s IT and build software that YWAM bases can use, so bases with little IT know-how can still run well and safely. Next to that I do what all staff do: the café, the kitchen, cleaning, the sound for worship nights and joining in with evangelism.

  4. The team

    YWAM staff don’t get a salary, so I’m building a team that sends me: in prayer, with a monthly gift and by following along. I leave friends, family and Ignite, the youth group I pour my heart into, so the people back home matter a lot.

Follow the journey

The road from here to Queenstown.

Moving to the other side of the world doesn’t happen in one go. These are the stretches of the road, and where I am on it right now.

  1. Getting ready

    Now, in the Netherlands (I’m here now)

    My NZeTA is applied for and my job is handed in. Now: building the team that sends me, sorting what I sell, store or take, and saying goodbye well.

  2. Departure

    January 2027

    From Schiphol to Queenstown, more than 18,000 km, with one checked bag, one backpack and a lot of people praying along.

  3. On staff

    After that

    Living and working on the base: IT and software, the everyday staff jobs and life with the young people. There, with YWAM’s help, I switch to a longer visa.
